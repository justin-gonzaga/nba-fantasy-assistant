## Gaps (no peer-reviewed support)

- **Topic 3 (stabilization rates specifically for NBA per-minute box-score stats):** the closest peer-reviewed
  work is R-75 (general "meta-metrics" stability framework, applied to NBA) and R-76 (preprint, FG% only).
  The specific practitioner concept of "X games until stat Y stabilizes" (R-77) has **no peer-reviewed NBA
  source**; it originates in sabermetrics (baseball, Russell Carleton) and was ported to basketball by
  bloggers (Blackport, Narsu, Medvedovsky). Use R-75's stability/discrimination framework as the
  peer-reviewed anchor and treat R-77 numbers only as an informative prior to be re-estimated on our own data.
- **Topic 5 (auction-specific valuation, "how to convert value to dollars"):** no peer-reviewed *basketball*
  source was found that gives a specific dollar-conversion formula (e.g., VORP-to-dollars scaling). R-80 is
  peer-reviewed and fantasy-basketball-specific but studies *bidding behavior*, not the value→dollar
  conversion formula itself. The dollar-conversion formula in common use (value over replacement, scaled to
  sum to the total league budget) is a **practitioner standard** (e.g., RotoWire/FantasyPros/DraftSharks
  auction-value guides found in search) with **no peer-reviewed source identified**. This should be stated
  explicitly in the ML plan as a practitioner-standard heuristic, not an academic result.
- **Topic 6 (replacement level / VORP formalization):** **not researched this pass** — flagged as
  `Unverified — pending`, needs a dedicated follow-up search (e.g., sabermetric WAR/replacement-level
  literature such as Baseball Prospectus VORP origins, and any basketball-specific replacement-level papers)
  before RSCH-005 can be considered closed.
- **Access-level caveat:** several entries above (R-73, R-74, R-78, R-80, R-81) were confirmed only via
  search-engine result snippets / cached abstracts because the publisher pages returned HTTP 403 to the
  fetch tool. Per the `ml.md` §0 access rule (mirrored from `ml-literature-review.md`), these cannot be the
  **sole** basis for a gated (G-19) decision until their full text is actually read; that follow-up read is
  outstanding.
