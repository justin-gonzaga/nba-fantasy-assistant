## Gaps (no peer-reviewed support found)
1. No study of **October main-roster exhibition minutes/starts** predicting NBA regular-season role (only Summer League, R-92; NFL results, R-93). DRAFT-008 must justify it empirically (the pre-registered growth test).
2. No peer-reviewed study of **news/depth-chart text predicting NBA role or breakouts**. A news reader must be justified by its own measured extraction accuracy and a backtest.
3. No paper on **look-ahead bias with GDELT/Wayback** specifically; use the general leakage/rolling-origin references (R-50, R-51, R-60) and snapshot timestamps.
4. FACTS Grounding (arXiv:2501.03200) was seen only in secondary snippets (Not verified, not cited); arXiv:2409.00369 appeared withdrawn/ambiguous (excluded).
