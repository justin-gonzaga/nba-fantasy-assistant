# Literature Verification — Part B (R-40 … R-66)

Method: for each entry, searched for the primary source (publisher/DOI landing page, JSTOR/Wiley/Taylor & Francis/
Springer/ACM/PMLR/NeurIPS/ProjectEuclid/OTexts), then attempted `WebFetch` on the landing page or an open-access
PDF/preprint hosted by the authors or a university. Several publisher landing pages (Taylor & Francis, Wiley,
ScienceDirect, ACM abstract page) returned HTTP 403 to the fetch tool (anti-bot/paywall); for those, the citation
and abstract summary below are drawn from the search engine's indexed snippet of that same primary page (marked
"abstract only, via indexed snippet" in the Access column) plus cross-checks against a second independent listing
(JSTOR/DOI resolver, EconPapers/RePEc, ACM DL, Semantic Scholar) to reduce mis-citation risk. No claim below relies
on a non-primary blog or listicle as its sole source.

Access date for all entries: **2026-09-25**.

## Flags for the plan

- **R-43 (Dawid 1984):** page range in `ml-literature-review.md` should be corrected from "278–292" to **278–290** (confirmed independently on Oxford Academic and Wiley/JSTOR). Low risk, but should be fixed before citing exact pages externally.
- **R-61 (Glasserman 2003):** could not independently confirm from a primary table-of-contents source that "Ch. 4" is specifically the variance-reduction chapter (WebFetch was blocked from Springer's TOC and I did not access a full copy). The book does cover variance reduction (including common random numbers) somewhere in its ~600 pages — treat the chapter number as **UNVERIFIED** until someone checks a physical/library copy or Google Books preview. Confidence: Medium on topical relevance, Low on the specific chapter citation.
- **R-45, R-47, R-63, R-64 (Platt 1999; Niculescu-Mizil & Caruana 2005; Sculley et al. 2015; Breck et al. 2017):** full text was not machine-readable via the WebFetch tool (binary PDF extraction failed even when the correct primary-source PDF URL was reached). Bibliographic details and key results are corroborated by at least two independent listings (publisher/ACM/NeurIPS + Semantic Scholar/Google Research/author page), so confidence in the citation is High, but the "key result" summaries for these four are built from abstract-level snippets rather than a full read — recommend a human skim of the PDFs already saved locally by the fetch tool if a stronger evidentiary bar is needed.
- **General note:** Many landing pages (Taylor & Francis, Wiley Online Library, ScienceDirect, ACM DL abstract pages) returned HTTP 403 to the automated fetch tool (bot-blocking), so "Access" for several rows is "abstract only, via indexed snippet" rather than a direct fetch of the publisher HTML. This is standard for these publishers from an unauthenticated tool and does not itself cast doubt on the citations (cross-checked against a second independent index in each case), but a reader with institutional access could upgrade these to directly-fetched abstracts/full text.
- No entries in this batch were **Not found** — all 22 (R-40–R-48, R-50–R-55, R-60–R-66) resolved to a primary publisher/proceedings/press page.
